The webinar helped me understand the importance of collecting, organizing and analysing data before making decisions. I also gained a better idea of how analytics can be used to identify patterns, understand trends and support real-world problem solving.

Key takeaway: Data becomes more valuable when we can turn it into meaningful insights and use those insights to make better decisions.

Why It Was Useful for Me

The session gave me a practical perspective beyond classroom learning. It encouraged me to explore Data Analytics further and understand how it can complement my interests in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

My Experience

Overall, the webinar was a valuable learning experience. I enjoyed gaining new knowledge, connecting the concepts with real-world applications and thinking about how I can develop my skills further.

Conclusion

This webinar was a small but meaningful step in my learning journey. I look forward to attending more sessions, building projects and continuously improving my technical skills in AI, ML and Data Analytics.
